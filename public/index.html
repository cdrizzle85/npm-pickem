How it works
1
Each week, pick the winner of seven games, a mix of college and NFL.
2
After 12 weeks, the person with the most correct picks takes the trophy.
3
Picks lock individually, right when each game kicks off, not all at once. You can still pick later games even after an earlier one has started.
4
Miss a game? No stress. Any pick you don't get in before kickoff defaults to the Coin Flip entry, a real 50/50 random pick made fresh every week, so you never fully miss out.
Want to see how Coin Flip is doing against everyone? It's on the standings board like any other player, a built in baseline for "what if you just guessed."
Additional rules
5
Ties. If teams are tied at the end of the year, they enter a bonus playoff Pick 'Em: additional weeks of picks until a single victor emerges.
6
Joining late. Any weeks missed before you join default to the Coin Flip entry's picks.
7
Postponed or canceled games. These don't count for anyone that week.
