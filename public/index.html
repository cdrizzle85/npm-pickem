How it works
1
Each week, pick the winner of seven games, a mix of college and NFL.
2
After 12 weeks, the person with the most correct picks takes the trophy.
3
All seven picks for the week lock together, at kickoff of the first game, not one game at a time. You can change your picks as many times as you want before then, but once that first game kicks off, the whole week is locked in.
4
Didn't submit before that deadline? You automatically get 2 grace points for the week, and the rest of that week's games count as losses.
Grace points exist so one missed week doesn't wreck your season, not to reward skipping picks, you're better off submitting real ones.
Additional rules
5
Ties. A tiebreaker will be determined at the end of the season, if necessary.
6
Joining late. Any weeks missed before you join are counted as 2 grace points each, same as any other missed week.
7
Postponed or canceled games. These don't count for anyone that week.
